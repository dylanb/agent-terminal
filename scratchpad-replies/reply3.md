The read now goes through a single descriptor opened `O_RDONLY | O_NOFOLLOW | O_NONBLOCK`: `O_NOFOLLOW` refuses a symlink at the path (→ `ELOOP` → `confinement`), `O_NONBLOCK` won't block on a FIFO, and `fstat` + read on that same fd close the stat↔read window a swapped inode would win (CWE-59/367). The size cap is enforced from the `fstat` on that fd, not a prior `statSync`.
